## Vision

It is my personal AI-powered operating system for building long-term wealth through real estate.

```
   Survey Number, Village, Owner, Developer, Contractor, Project,

   Long-term storage · Semantic search · Vector embeddings
   Document storage · Relationship storage
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         AI Intelligence Layer (Claude Code)
   Research · Analysis · Summarisation · Scoring
   Relationship reasoning · Market reasoning
   Opportunity detection · Report generation · Learning assistant
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                            Application Layer
   Dashboard · Search · Daily Briefing · Knowledge Graph · CRM
   Property Explorer · Builder Explorer · Investment Committee
   Decision Journal · Portfolio
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              Final Output
        One recommendation · One opportunity · One action
                          Every day.
```

## Modules

### Module 1 — Knowledge Vault

Atlas remembers everything. Store:

- Notes, voice notes
- WhatsApp exports, emails
- PDFs, maps, survey sketches
- Images, brochures, videos
- OCR output
- Site visits, meeting notes

Every document becomes searchable.

### Module 2 — Relationship Intelligence

Maintain profiles for: Broker, Builder, Architect, Lawyer, Surveyor, Contractor, Engineer, CA, Banker, Investor, Land Owner.

Store: Contact, Projects, Area, Trust, Notes, Strengths, Weaknesses, Mutual contacts, Follow-up history, Conversation summaries.

> Atlas should remind me who to call.

### Module 3 — Property Intelligence

Every property becomes an evolving record. Store: Location, GPS, Road width, Facing, Price, Price history, Builder, Broker, Images, Videos, Timeline, Nearby schools, Nearby hospitals, Metro, Commercial hubs, Infrastructure, Ownership notes.

> History should never be overwritten.

### Module 4 — Builder Intelligence

Track: Projects, Completion history, Delay history, Construction quality, Reviews, RERA, Litigation, Pricing, Preferred locations, Financial stress signals.

> Atlas should answer: "Would I trust this builder?"

### Module 5 — Broker Intelligence

Atlas should know:

- Who finds land?
- Who finds apartments?
- Who specialises in JD?
- Who has distressed inventory?
- Who wastes my time?

Every broker receives a trust score.

### Module 6 — Market Intelligence

Continuously monitor: BBMP, BDA, BMRDA, RERA, KIADB, Metro, Road projects, Master Plan, Government notifications.

> Every update should answer: "What opportunity does this create?"

### Module 7 — Property Discovery

Data sources: Google Search, Google Maps, Official RERA, Government websites, Builder websites, Emails, WhatsApp, Manual site visits, Playwright automation, Apify (where useful).

> Atlas should not depend on a single data source.

### Module 8 — Opportunity Engine

This is the heart. Atlas constantly searches for:

- Price reductions
- Relisted properties
- Long time on market
- Builder distress
- Owner distress
- Corner sites
- Redevelopment
- Joint Development
- Land aggregation
- Commercial conversion
- Road widening
- Upcoming metro
- Undervalued micro markets

Each opportunity gets: **Opportunity Score · Reason · Suggested Action · Confidence**

### Module 9 — Site Visit Assistant

**Before visit:** Generate checklist.

**During visit:** Capture Voice, Photos, Videos, GPS, Notes.

**After visit:** Generate Summary, Risks, Questions, Negotiation ideas, Comparable properties, Missing information.

### Module 10 — Investment Committee

Every investment goes through AI review. Questions:

- Why buy? Why not buy?
- Worst case? Best case?
- Exit strategy?
- Capital needed?
- Holding period?
- Legal risks? Construction risks?
- Rental potential?
- Alternative opportunities?

> Atlas should argue both sides.

### Module 11 — Decision Journal

Every investment decision should be stored.

**Before:** Reason, Expected outcome, Expected return, Risks, Confidence.

**After:** Actual outcome, Lessons, Mistakes.

> Atlas should identify recurring biases.

### Module 12 — Portfolio Dashboard

Track: Assets, Loans, Cash flow, Rental income, Net worth, Equity, Returns, Portfolio allocation, Goal progress.

### Module 13 — Learning Engine

Every week Atlas should teach me one concept. Examples: Title verification, RTC, Mutation, EC, Khata, FSI, FAR, Construction costs, Joint Development, Negotiation, Finance, Taxation, Valuation.

> Atlas should gradually turn me into an expert.

### Module 14 — Daily Briefing

Every morning generate: Top opportunities, Builder news, Government updates, Relationship reminders, Market movements, Learning recommendation, Suggested calls, Suggested site visits, Priority list.

> No noise. Only actions.
